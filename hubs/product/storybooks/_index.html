How nhetrinh
is built.

A very small macOS browser: Apple's WebKit inside a Swift + AppKit shell, with tabs in a sidebar. These pages show its design for a human reader: what a browser must have, the parts nhetrinh is made of, and what runs where while you browse.

The one-line design. nhetrinh draws the window, the tabs and the address bar. Apple's WebKit does the hard part: it fetches pages, runs their JavaScript and draws them, in its own processes. nhetrinh decides when a page may cost memory, and gives it back when you are not using it.
