3 · Must, should, and nhetrinh's own

MUSTA window, an address bar, one tab that shows a page, back, forward, reload, and HTTPS. Without these, it is not a browser.
SHOULDMany tabs, tabs that come back after a quit, error pages, downloads, file uploads, find in page, zoom, and links from other apps.
NHETRINHA tab loads only when you show it. A tab you do not use gives its memory back. Groups of tabs. A history list. Sign-ins kept. A 1 MB app.

Why not build our own engine?

An engine has millions of lines of code. A small team cannot write one.

Chrome-based browsers carry their own engine. That costs hundreds of megabytes.

WebKit is already on every Mac, and Apple keeps it up to date. Sites work, because Safari uses it too.
