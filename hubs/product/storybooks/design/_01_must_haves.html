3 · Must, should, and nhetrinh's own

MUSTA window, an address bar, one tab that shows a page through an engine, back / forward / reload, HTTPS. Without these it is not a browser.
SHOULDMany tabs, tabs that survive a quit, error pages with Retry, downloads, uploads, find in page, zoom, page dialogs, links from other apps.
NHETRINHTabs load only when shown; idle tabs give their memory back; nested tab groups; a history dropdown; sign-ins kept across launches; a 1 MB app.

Why borrow the engine? An engine is millions of lines (WebKit, Blink, Gecko). Writing one is out of reach for a small browser, and shipping one (as Chromium-based browsers do) costs hundreds of megabytes. WebKit is already on every Mac, updated by Apple, and the same engine Safari uses, so sites work.
