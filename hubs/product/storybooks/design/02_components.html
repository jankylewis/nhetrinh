3 · And one tool outside the app

The readback (nhetrinh-readback) is a separate program. It launches the installed app the way you do, drives it through a private socket (the probe) and through the Dock, and gives a verdict per step. It is how a change is proven on the real app, not only in tests. It is never part of the app you use: the probe exists only when the app is started with NHT_READBACK=1.
