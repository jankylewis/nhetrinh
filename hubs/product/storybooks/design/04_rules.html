04 · DESIGN RULES

Lightness first,
speed second.

nhetrinh exists to be light. Four numbers bound it, and a handful of rules keep every change inside them.

1 · The lightness budget

MeasureLimitWhy it holds
The app's own process40 MBThe page content lives in WebKit's processes, not here
App + WebKit helpers110 MBOnly shown tabs load; idle tabs unload
The app bundle1 MBThe engine is the Mac's; no third-party code
CPU when idle2 %No timers that poll; work happens on events

Today: the bundle measures 1,048 KB, a little over 1 MB, with more features waiting on other branches. The headmate decides between a new limit and cuts (the plan's open question). A change that breaks the budget stops until the cost is removed or a new number is accepted.

3 · The rules that keep it right

TESTS FIRSTEvery rule is a test before it is code. A bug fix starts with a test that fails, and a test that cannot fail is itself a bug.
READBACKDone means used: the readback drives the installed app as you would and checks what it shows. "Merged" and "tests green" are not done.
HUBSEvery bug, failure, lesson and feature is written down in hubs/ in the same change, with who found it and what proves it.
YOUR MACHINEBuilds and tests run gently (nice), one heavy job at a time; the app you are using is never restarted or replaced without your go.
PUBLICThe repository is public: no secrets, names, work details or personal paths in any file. Sign-ins stay in the Keychain; cookie values are never logged.
