2 · When it goes wrong

NO NETWORKStep 6 fails: nhetrinh shows its error page in the tab, with Retry. A bad certificate fails the same way; there is no "proceed anyway".
PASSWORDThe site asks for a user and password (HTTP Basic): a sign-in sheet on the window.
CRASHThe page's process dies: only that tab is affected. nhetrinh logs it and reloads the tab.
OTHER APPA mailto: or meeting link: a sheet asks first, then macOS opens the app. Nothing leaves without your Open.
