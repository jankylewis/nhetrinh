2 · When something goes wrong

NO NETWORKStep 6 fails. nhetrinh shows its error page with Retry. A bad certificate fails the same way. There is no "continue anyway" button.
PASSWORDThe site asks for a user name and password. nhetrinh shows a sign-in box.
CRASHThe page helper stops. Only that tab is hit. nhetrinh writes a log line and loads the tab again.
OTHER APPA link to Mail or a meeting app. A box asks you first. Then macOS opens the app. Nothing leaves without your OK.
