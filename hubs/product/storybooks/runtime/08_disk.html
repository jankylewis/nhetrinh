08 · ON DISK

What is kept,
and where.

Four small files of nhetrinh's own, one Keychain item, and WebKit's data store. Nothing is sent anywhere: no account, no sync, no telemetry.

1 · The places

CLICK A BOX

~/Library/Application Support/nhetrinh/
session.jsonyour tabs and groups
prefs.jsonSettings Hub, zoom per site
history.sqlitevisits, for the dropdown
running.markerwas the last quit clean?
KEYCHAINone item: kept sign-ins
WEBKIT DATA STOREcookies · cache · site storage
~/Downloadsyour downloads, quarantined

Amber: nhetrinh writes these itself. Violet: macOS keeps it, encrypted. Green: WebKit manages it. The readback runs in its own profile, with its own folder and store, so it never touches these.

On a branch, not in your build yet: profiles (P10) move these files into nhetrinh/Default/, one folder per profile, with a one-time move of your current files at the first launch.

2 · A launch

1 · WINDOWThe window and the menu appear first, empty.
2 · SIGN-INSIf the last quit was clean, the kept cookies are read from the Keychain and set back into WebKit, with a short wait so they land first.
3 · TABSsession.json is read; every tab comes back unloaded; the selected one loads. A file that cannot be read is set aside as session.bad.json, never deleted.
4 · LINKSLinks that arrived during the launch (you clicked one in another app) open as new tabs.

3 · A quit

1 · TABSsession.json written at once.
2 · SIGN-INSWebKit is asked for the session cookies; they go into the one Keychain item. The app waits for that answer, then ends.
3 · CLEANThe marker is cleared: the next launch knows this quit was clean.
