07 · A TAB'S LIFE

Four states,
one costly.

A tab is cheap: an address, a title, an icon. Only a loaded tab holds a page in a WebKit process. nhetrinh's whole lightness story is about keeping tabs out of that state when you are not looking.

1 · The states

CLICK A STATE

NOT YET LOADEDrestored or opened in the background · no web view · ~0 MB
▼you show it (click, ⌘1-9, ⌃⇥)
LOADEDweb view + its page process · the costly state
▼idle 10 min · Put to Idle · memory short
▲you show it again
IDLE (UNLOADED)dimmed in the sidebar · history kept · ~0 MB
▼you close it (from any state)
CLOSEDkept for ⌘⇧T with its place in the groups

Read the arrows as rules. A tab moves down to IDLE only when nhetrinh is sure you lose nothing; it moves up to LOADED only when you look at it.

2 · Who may not go idle

SHOWNThe tab in view never goes idle. "Put Tab to Idle" on it first shows the nearest other tab.
PLAYINGA tab playing sound or video stays loaded: WebKit is asked first.
IN A CALLA tab using the camera or the microphone stays loaded.
EVERY OTHERBackground, silent, unused past the idle minutes (10 by default; a minute when macOS warns of memory; at once when memory is critical).
